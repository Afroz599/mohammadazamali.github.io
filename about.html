Professional Profile

Educator • Researcher • Visionary

Academician & Social Reformer

Dr. Mohammad Azam Ali is working as an Assistant Professor of English in the Department of Basic Sciences and Humanities at Vaagdevi Engineering College.

He obtained his M.A., M.Ed., and Ph.D. in English from Kakatiya University. With over a decade of teaching experience at the undergraduate level, he has been actively involved in academic instruction, research, student mentoring, and personality development initiatives.

Mission Statement

"To transform students into responsible citizens with strong moral values, leadership qualities, social responsibility, and national consciousness."

Academic Specialization & Research

His core areas of academic expertise encompass a broad literary and instructional scope:

British Literature American Literature Indian Writing in English Post-Colonial Literature English Language Teaching (ELT)

Dr. Azam Ali has published several research papers in UGC CARE-listed journals and peer-reviewed international journals with notable impact factors. He is also the author of the published volume, "Indian Youth Literature: Mapping Young Minds in Changing India." In addition, he has participated in and presented cutting-edge research papers at numerous national and international seminars and conferences.

National Recognitions

Apart from his academic contributions, Dr. Azam Ali is widely recognized for his outstanding service in the fields of youth empowerment and community development.

Indira Gandhi NSS National Award

Conferred by the then President of India, Pranab Mukherjee, for the year 2012–13 under the Best NSS Volunteer category.

National Youth Award

Received India’s prestigious national decoration for his exemplary contributions towards community service and youth empowerment.

Social Impact & Community Leadership

Chamak Foundation (“Chamak Padhai Se”)

As the Founder and President, Dr. Azam Ali leads this noted non-profit organization dedicated to the educational empowerment of underprivileged students. Through the foundation, he has been actively working to support poor and marginalized individuals by providing:

Academic Guidance & Mentoring
Career Counselling
Motivational Support
Financial Assistance for Meritorious Students

As part of his social commitment towards educational development, he has initiated library programs and book distribution activities in schools and rural communities to promote reading habits. The foundation also regularly organizes structural awareness programs and personality development sessions.

Dr. Azam Ali is also a renowned motivational speaker who has delivered hundreds of inspirational lectures across schools and colleges, motivating and empowering student demographics through his precise guidance and transformative institutional vision.
