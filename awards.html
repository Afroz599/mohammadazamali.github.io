Indira Gandhi NSS National Award

Presented by the President of India | 2013

One of the highest civilian honors for youth and social service in India. Conferred under the Best Volunteer Category for the year 2012‑13 by the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports, Govt. of India, and honored at Rashtrapati Bhavan, New Delhi.

1. Indira Gandhi NSS National Award

Awarded under the Best Volunteer Category for the year 2012‑13 by the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports, Govt. of India. Presented by the President of India at Rashtrapati Bhavan.

2. National Youth Award

Conferred for the year 2017‑18 by the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports, Govt. of India, at Vigyan Bhavan, New Delhi, for deep dedication to social transformation.

3. Maulana Azad National Award

Conferred in 2018 by the Bahujana Sahitya Academy, India, honoring outstanding performance milestones and exceptional public contributions.

4. Rashtriya Gaurav Samman Award

Awarded in 2018 by the National Youth Awardees Federation of India, recognizing youth leadership excellence and commitment to nation-building.

5. Ambedkar National Award

Presented in 2019 by the Dalita Sahithya Academy, New Delhi, honoring continuous community welfare services and impactful social work designs.

6. State‑level Pratibha Puraskar Award

Conferred in 2008 by the Department of Higher Education, Govt. of Andhra Pradesh, for securing the absolute highest marks framework across Intermediate education.

7. State Best NSS Volunteer Award

Conferred for the year 2012-13 by the Department of Higher Education, Govt. of Andhra Pradesh, acknowledging outstanding performance across state university levels.

8. Telangana State Ideal Teacher Award

Conferred in 2021 by the All India Ideal Teachers Association, marking pedagogical commitment, excellence in teaching, and student mentorship.

9. Mahatma Gandhi Sadbhavana Award

Conferred in 2023 by the Sadhvana Forum, Karimnagar, celebrating continuous dedication to communal harmony, social ethics, and public welfare pathways.
